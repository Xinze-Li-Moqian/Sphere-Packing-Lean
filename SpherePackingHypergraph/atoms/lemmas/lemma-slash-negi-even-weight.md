# Lemma — lemma:slash-negI-even-weight

## Statement

> [!lemma] lemma:slash-negI-even-weight
> For even $k$, $F|_{k}(-I) = F$.

**In terms of**: [[def-slash-operator]].

## Proof

> [!note]- Proof (click to expand)
> Follows from the definition of the [[def-slash-operator|slash operator]]: $(F|_{k}(-I))(z) = (-1)^{-k}F((-I)z) = F(z)$.

**Uses**: [[def-slash-operator]].
