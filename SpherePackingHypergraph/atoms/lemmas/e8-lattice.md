# Lemma — E8-Lattice

## Statement

> [!lemma] E8-Lattice
> $\Lambda_8$ is an additive subgroup of $\mathbb{R}^8$.

## Proof

> [!note]- Proof (click to expand)
> Trivially follows from that $\Lambda_8 \subseteq \mathbb{R}^8$ is the $\mathbb{Z}$-span of $\mathcal{B}_8$ and hence an additive group.
