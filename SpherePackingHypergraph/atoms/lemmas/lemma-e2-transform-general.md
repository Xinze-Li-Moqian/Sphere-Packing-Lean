# Lemma — lemma:E2-transform-general

## Statement

> [!lemma] lemma:E2-transform-general
> $$
> (cz + d)^{-2} E_2\left(\frac{az + b}{cx + d}\right) = E_2(z) - \frac{6ic}{\pi (cz + d)}, \quad \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d\end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z}).
> $$

## Proof

> [!note]- Proof (click to expand)
> Use the fact that $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is generated by $S$ and $T$. Then [[eq-e2-transform-general]] follows from [[eq-e2-s-transform]] and $E_2|_T = E_2$.
