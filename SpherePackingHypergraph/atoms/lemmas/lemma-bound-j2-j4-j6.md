# Lemma — lemma:bound-J2-J4-J6

## Statement

> [!lemma] lemma:bound-J2-J4-J6
> There exist $C_1, C_2 > 0$ such that
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> |J_2(r)|, |J_4(r)| &\le C_1 e^{-\pi r} \\
> |J_6(r)| &\le C_2 \frac{e^{\pi (r + 1)}}{r + 1}
> \end{aligned}
> $$
