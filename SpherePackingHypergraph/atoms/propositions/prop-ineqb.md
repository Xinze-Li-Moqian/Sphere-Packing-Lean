# Proposition — prop:ineqB

## Statement

> [!proposition] prop:ineqB
> Consider the function $B:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb{C}$ defined as
> $$
> B(t) := -t^2\phi_0(i/t)+\frac{36}{\pi^2}\,\psi_I(it)
> $$
> Then
> $$
> B(t) > 0
> $$
> for all $t > 0$.
