# Proposition — prop:ineqA

## Statement

> [!proposition] prop:ineqA
> Consider the function $A:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb{C}$ defined as
> $$
> A(t):=-t^2\phi_0(i/t)-\frac{36}{\pi^2}\,\psi_I(it).
> $$
> Then
> $$
> A(t) < 0
> $$
> for all $t > 0$.

**Uses**: [[cor-ineqanew]], [[lemma-f-g-phi-psi-identities]], [[lemma-f-g-pos]], [[lemma-ineqabnew-equiv]].
