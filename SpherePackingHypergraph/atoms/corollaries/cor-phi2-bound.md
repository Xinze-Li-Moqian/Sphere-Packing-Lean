# Corollary — cor:phi2-bound

## Statement

> [!corollary] cor:phi2-bound
> There exists a constant $C_{-2} > 0$ such that
> $$
> |\phi_{-2}(z)| \le C_{-2}
> $$
> for all $z$ with $\Im z > 1/2$.

**Uses**: [[def-phi4-phi2-phi0]], [[lemma-ek-fourier]], [[lemma-mod-div-disc-bound]].
