# Definition — SpherePacking.density

## Statement

> [!definition] SpherePacking.density
> We define the *density* of a packing $\mathcal{P}$ as the limit superior
> 
> $$\Delta_{\mathcal{P}}:=\limsup\limits_{R\to\infty}\Delta_{\mathcal{P}}(R).$$
