# Definition — def:Ek

## Statement

> [!definition] def:Ek
> For an even integer $k\geq 4$ we define the *weight $k$ Eisenstein series* as
> $$
> E_k(z):=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{(c,d)\in\mathbb{Z}^2, (c,d)=1}(cz+d)^{-k}.$$
