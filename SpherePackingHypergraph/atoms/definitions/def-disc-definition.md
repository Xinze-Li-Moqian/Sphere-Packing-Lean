# Definition — def:disc-definition

## Statement

> [!definition] def:disc-definition
> The *discriminant form* $\Delta(z)$ is given by
> $$
> \Delta(z) = e^{2 \pi i z} \prod_{n \ge 1} (1 - e^{2 \pi i n z})^{24}.
> $$
