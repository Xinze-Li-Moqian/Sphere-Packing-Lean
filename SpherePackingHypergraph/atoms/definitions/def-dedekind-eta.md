# Definition — def:dedekind_eta

## Statement

> [!definition] def:dedekind_eta
> The Dedekind eta function is defined as
> $$
> \eta(z) = q^{1/24} \prod_{n \ge 1} (1 - q^n)
> $$
> where $q = e^{2\pi i z}$.
