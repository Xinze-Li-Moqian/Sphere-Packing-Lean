# Definition — def:dual-lattice

## Statement

> [!definition] def:dual-lattice
> The *dual lattice* of a [[iszlattice|lattice]] $\Lambda$ is the set
> 
> $$\Lambda^* := \left\{ v \in \mathbb{R}^d \; \middle| \; \forall l \in \Lambda, \left\langle v,l \right\rangle \in \mathbb{Z} \right\}$$
