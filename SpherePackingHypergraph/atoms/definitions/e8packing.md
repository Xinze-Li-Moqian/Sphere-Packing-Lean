# Definition — E8Packing

## Statement

> [!definition] E8Packing
> The *$E_8$ sphere packing* is the ([[periodicspherepacking|periodic]]) [[spherepackingballs|sphere packing]] with separation $\sqrt{2}$, whose set of centres is $\Lambda_8$.
