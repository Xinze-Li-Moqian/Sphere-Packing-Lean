# Definition — def:serre-der

## Statement

> [!definition] def:serre-der
> For $k \in \mathbb{R}$, define the weight $k$ Serre derivative $\partial_{k}$ of a [[def-mk|modular form]] $F$ as
> $$
> \partial_{k}F := F' - \frac{k}{12} E_2 F.
> $$
