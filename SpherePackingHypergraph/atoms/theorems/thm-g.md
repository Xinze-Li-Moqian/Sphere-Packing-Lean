# Theorem — thm:g

## Statement

> [!theorem] thm:g
> There exists a radial [[def-schwartz-space|Schwartz function]] $g:\mathbb{R}^8\to\mathbb{R}$ which satisfies:
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> g(x)&\leq 0\mbox{ for } \|x\|\geq \sqrt{2} \\
> \widehat{g}(x)&\geq0\mbox{ for all } x\in\mathbb{R}^8\\
> g(0)&=\widehat{g}(0)=1.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
