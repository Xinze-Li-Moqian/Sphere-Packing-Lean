# Theorem — thm:nonpos_wt

## Statement

> [!theorem] thm:nonpos_wt
> Let $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ with $k < 0$. Then $M_k(\Gamma_1) = \{0\}$ and moreover $\dim M_0(\Gamma(1)) = 1$.
